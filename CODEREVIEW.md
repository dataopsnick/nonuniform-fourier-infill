### Top Issues

1. **Silent corruption of NUDFT / imputation results in `torch_kernels.py`**  
   - **Missing `valid_idx` return** when degenerate columns are dropped: downstream callers have no way to align results with original frequency components, leading to wrong imputations.  
   - **Unsorted timestamps produce an incorrect Nyquist frequency** (`np.nanmin(p_n) > 0` false → default to 1.0), silently distorting the entire frequency axis.  
   - **No NaN/Inf input validation** – NaN/Inf in timestamps or data propagate through the Fourier matrix, causing NaN results or cryptic `LinAlgError`.  
   - **Ill‑conditioned normal equations** – explicitly forming `A^H @ A + alpha*I` squares the condition number, losing precision on realistic data.  
   - **Empty `alphas` list** causes `IndexError` (line 179) because the `alphas is None` guard misses an empty iterable.  
   *Paths:* `nonuniform-fourier-infill/nufi/kernels/torch_kernels.py`

2. **Data races and integrity bugs in `agent.py`**  
   - **TOCTOU race in path sanitization** – `realpath` resolved at check time, but symlinks between check and write can redirect writes outside allowed directories.  
   - **TOCTOU in `revert_to_version`** – `list_versions()` released lock; another thread can delete the snapshot before `pd.read_csv` executes.  
   - **No integrity checks on snapshots** – a truncated CSV is loaded silently with corrupted data.  
   - **Orphaned CSV snapshots on crash** – file written before log entry; process death leaves orphaned files that accumulate forever.  
   *Path:* `nonuniform-fourier-infill/nufi/agent.py`

3. **Imputation correctness bugs in `impute.py`**  
   - **Covariance compensation dimension mismatch** – when `covariance_compensation()` drops degenerate columns, the returned `lu/d/perm` are smaller than expected, causing mis‑scaling or outright crashes.  
   - **Stochastic scaling inconsistency** – observed values are not scaled by `cov_scale`, while imputed values and noise are, leading to biased reconstructions.  
   *Path:* `nonuniform-fourier-infill/nufi/impute.py`

4. **Benchmark biases and fragility (`tests/benchmark.py`)**

   - **Narrow exception handling** – only catches `ValueError, RuntimeError, ImportError`, ignoring `LinAlgError` or `MemoryError` from other methods.  
   - **All‑NaN channel edge case** – empty channel crashes downstream routines.
